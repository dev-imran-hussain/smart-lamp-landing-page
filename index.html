Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the AuraThinker™ Smart Desk Lamp.

How does the auto-adaptive ambient light sensor work? ▼

The built-in ambient light sensor automatically adjusts brightness based on surrounding conditions. As daylight fades or room lights shift, AuraThinker smoothly recalibrates its output to maintain comfortable, balanced lighting across your workspace.

How is the lamp powered? Can I connect it to a laptop or power bank? ▼

Yes! AuraThinker is powered via a universal USB Type-C port (5V/3A). You can power it from any USB wall charger, your laptop's Thunderbolt/USB-C port, or an external power bank for clean wire management across your desk.

Do I need the mobile companion app to operate the lamp? ▼

No. You can switch between all 3 preset modes (Focus, Read, Sleep) and steplessly adjust brightness directly using the touch-sensitive base. The optional iOS & Android app is available if you prefer setting custom schedules, study timers, or remote control.

What is the shipping timeframe and warranty policy in India? ▼
